How the palettes are generated

Each palette fixes a hue and chroma and ramps lightness across the 13 tones in the OKLCH colour space, clamping chroma back into the sRGB gamut per stop. Secondary and tertiary reuse the source hue (tertiary rotated +60°) at reduced chroma; the neutrals are the source hue at near-zero chroma. It is a perceptual approximation, not a port of Google's HCT algorithm — no dependencies, ~40 lines of colour maths. The role mapping (tone 40 for primary in light, tone 80 in dark, and so on) mirrors src/tokens/tokens.css exactly, so a copied block is a drop-in replacement.
