How the palettes are generated

Each palette fixes a hue and chroma and ramps lightness across the 13 tones in the OKLCH colour space, clamping chroma back into the sRGB gamut per stop. Secondary and tertiary reuse the source hue at reduced chroma, rotated by the harmony setting (tertiary +60° by default); the neutrals are the source hue — or a preset's neutral tint — at near-zero chroma; the error hue is anchored to the MD3 baseline. It is a perceptual approximation, not a port of Google's HCT algorithm — no dependencies, ~50 lines of colour maths. The role mapping (tone 40 for primary in light, tone 80 in dark, and so on) mirrors src/tokens/tokens.css exactly, so a copied block is a drop-in replacement.

Preset themes seed the engine with a project's signature accent colour (plus a neutral tint and a light/dark preference). They are tonal systems derived from each theme, not reproductions of it. Every preset is credited to its origin above the palettes.
